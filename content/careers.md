# Careers at *Sri Siam*

Lead: We sometimes welcome Thai massage and spa therapists to our team in Greenwich. There is no application form: send us a message on WhatsApp and we will take it from there.

- Button (primary, tag CR): Message us on WhatsApp

Eyebrow: How to apply

## Say hello on WhatsApp

### Send us a message
Tap the button to open WhatsApp with a short message ready to send. Tell us a little about yourself and the kind of work you are looking for.

### Tell us about your experience
Mention the treatments you offer and where you have worked or trained. If you hold any qualifications, you can tell us about them in the same chat.

### We will reply when we can
We do not hold a fixed number of roles, and openings come up from time to time. If there is nothing right now we will say so, and we are glad to hear from you either way.
