# Privacy notice

Last updated {{privacy_updated_date}}.

Sri Siam Thai Therapy & Wellness, {{address}}, is the data controller for the personal details described here. You can reach us about anything in this notice at {{email}}.

## This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or any other tracking. It does not ask you to fill in a form or create an account. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard technical server logs under GitHub's own privacy statement.

The booking buttons on this site open WhatsApp on your device with a short message already typed. Nothing is sent until you press send, and this website does not know whether you did.

## When you message us on WhatsApp

When you message us, WhatsApp (provided by Meta) handles the message under its own terms and privacy policy, as with any WhatsApp chat. We see your phone number, the name on your WhatsApp profile and what you write to us.

We use those details to answer you, to book your treatment and to tell you about that booking, for example to confirm the time or to let you know if something changes.

Some replies on WhatsApp may be sent by an automated booking assistant that works for us, and a member of the team can step in at any time. Ask in the chat if you would like to speak to a person.

## Your booking

To run your booking we keep your name, your phone number, the treatment and length you chose and the date and time. We keep our appointment diary in Treatwell, so those details are stored there under Treatwell's own privacy terms.

If you tell us about your health so that we can check a treatment is right for you, we use that information only for that purpose.

## Deposits and payment

Deposit and advance payments are taken by a secure payment link from our payment provider. We do not see or store your full card details. Payments at the shop are taken by card or in cash.

## How long we keep your details

We keep booking details for as long as we need them to run and account for your bookings, and then delete them. WhatsApp messages stay in our chat history unless you ask us to delete them.

## Your rights

You can ask us what details we hold about you, ask us to correct them or ask us to delete them. Message us on WhatsApp or email {{email}}. If you are not happy with how we handle your details, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

## Changes to this notice

If we change how we handle your details, we update this page and the date at the top.
