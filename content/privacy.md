## Linking a booking to one of our ads

If you came to this website from one of our Google ads and clicked Accept, the code at the end of your WhatsApp message is slightly longer, such as [web-AD-K7M2Q9XP]. The website works it out from Google's reference for your ad click, which is in the web address you arrived on.

When you tap a WhatsApp button, the website sends that code and the ad click reference to our booking system, which Gumon Technology runs for us. With them it sends which button you tapped, when your visit started and which version of the cookie banner you accepted. It does not send your name, your phone number or your message. As with any request over the internet, our booking system also receives your IP address and details of your browser. It uses your IP address only briefly to prevent abuse and does not store it with the click reference; {{click_log_retention}}. This website does not store the click reference on your device for this purpose; the Google Ads cookies described above are separate. If you clicked Reject, did not choose, or did not come from one of our ads, nothing is sent.

When your message reaches us, the code links that ad click to your WhatsApp conversation with us and to any booking you make. If you go on to book or visit us, we use it to tell Google Ads, through Google's conversion upload, that the ad click led to a booking or a paid visit. For a booking we send Google the click reference, that a booking was made and its date and time. For a paid visit we send the click reference, the date and time of the visit and the amount you paid in pounds. Each goes with whether you gave consent. We never send Google your name, your phone number, your email address, your messages, the treatment you chose or anything about your health. Google handles this under its own privacy policy and may process it outside the UK. Our booking system may hold the click reference outside the UK, in the same way as your messages (see below).

We delete the ad click reference {{click_retention}}. Without the click reference, the source of your enquiry (that it came from a Google ad, which campaign, and the date) stays in your customer record for as long as you are a customer, and we keep counts that contain no personal details. We do all of this only with your consent, which you give by clicking Accept.

You can change your mind at any time with Cookie settings: if you choose Reject, the website stops sending click references at once and your messages end with [web-AD] again. To have a click reference we already hold deleted, or to stop a booking being reported to Google, ask us in the WhatsApp chat or email {{email}} before it is reported to Google: a report already sent to Google cannot be withdrawn there. You can also delete the code from the WhatsApp message before you send it; your booking is not affected. The click reference already sent is then not linked to a booking, and is deleted as described above.

## When you message us on WhatsApp

When you message us, WhatsApp (provided by Meta) handles the message under its own terms and privacy policy, as with any WhatsApp chat. We see your phone number, the name on your WhatsApp profile and what you write to us.

We use those details to answer you, to book your treatment and to tell you about that booking, for example to confirm the time or to let you know if something changes.

Some replies on WhatsApp may be sent by an automated booking assistant. It is provided by Gumon Technology, which handles your messages on our behalf. A member of the team can step in at any time; ask in the chat if you would like to speak to a person.

Gumon Technology and the companies it uses to run the assistant may process your messages, including any health details you choose to share, outside the UK, including in Thailand and the United States. You can ask us for details of the safeguards that apply.

## Your booking

To run your booking we keep your name, your phone number, the treatment and length you chose and the date and time. We keep our appointment diary in Treatwell, so those details are stored there under Treatwell's own privacy terms.

If you choose to tell us about your health so that we can check a treatment is right for you, we use that information only for that purpose, on the basis of your explicit consent. You can withdraw that consent at any time by telling us.

## Marketing messages

If you book or visit, we may send you occasional news and offers about similar treatments by WhatsApp, and by email if you gave us your email address when booking or paying, as our booking terms explain. We do this because you are our customer (the 'soft opt-in' allowed by the UK's Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations), and only for our own treatments. You can opt out at any time: reply STOP to any WhatsApp message, or use the unsubscribe link in any email. We keep a record that you opted out so we do not message you again.

## Deposits and payment

Deposits and advance payments are taken through a secure payment link from Wix Payments. We do not see or store your full card details. Payments at the shop are taken by card or in cash.

## Why we are allowed to use your details

We use your details to take and run your booking because that is needed to provide the treatment you asked for. We answer enquiries that are not yet a booking because we have a legitimate interest in replying to people who contact us. We keep payment records because the law requires us to keep accounts. We send news and offers about our own treatments to people who have booked or visited, because we have a legitimate interest in keeping our customers informed, and you can opt out at any time. Health information is only used with your explicit consent, as described above.

## How long we keep your details

We keep booking details and WhatsApp messages only for as long as we need them to run and account for your bookings, and then delete them. If you have booked or visited, we keep your name and contact details to send you news and offers until you opt out. When you opt out, we keep only the contact detail and the date, so that we do not message you again. Payment records are kept for as long as the law requires for our accounts.

## Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the details we hold about you, and ask us to correct them, delete them, limit how we use them, or send them to you or someone else in a common format. You can object to us using your details on the basis of our legitimate interests, and you can withdraw any consent you have given. Message us on WhatsApp or email {{email}}. If you are not happy with how we handle your details, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
