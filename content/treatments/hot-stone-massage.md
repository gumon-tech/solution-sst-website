## what-to-expect

## What to expect

The stones are smooth, dark basalt, warmed in water. Your therapist checks how the warmth feels for you as they go, places a line of them along your back and sometimes rests smaller ones in your palms or on your legs, then uses other stones, oiled and warm, as an extension of their hands.

The feeling is of deep, steady warmth spreading from wherever a stone rests, followed by long, gliding strokes that carry that warmth across the back, shoulders and legs. The pressure is medium, and because warm muscles accept pressure more easily the work can go deeper without feeling hard. If a stone ever feels too warm, say so and it is lifted at once.

## focus

### What it focuses on
- The back and shoulders, where the stones are laid
- Legs, arms and feet with gliding stone strokes
- Warmth and pressure together, in a slow rhythm
- Medium pressure that your therapist adjusts with you

### Good to know
- Uses oil, so you undress to your comfort level and are covered with a towel throughout
- Tell your therapist straight away if a stone feels too warm; it is lifted at once
- If you are pregnant, or have a health condition, an injury or a recent operation, tell us before you book so the team can confirm the right treatment. If in doubt, ask your doctor first.
- The longer option gives time for both the placed stones and a full body massage

## faq

### How hot are the stones in a hot stone massage?
The stones are warmed in water to a comfortable heat, so they feel deeply warm rather than hot. Your therapist asks how they feel, and any stone can be lifted straight away.

### What kind of stones are used?
Smooth basalt stones. Basalt is a dark volcanic rock that holds warmth well, which is why it is the traditional choice for this treatment.

### Is hot stone massage a full body massage?
Yes. Stones are placed along the back and used in the therapist's hands to massage the back, shoulders, legs and arms with oil. Choose the length that suits you from the options above.
