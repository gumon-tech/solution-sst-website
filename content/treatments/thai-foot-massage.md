## what-to-expect

## What to expect

You stay dressed; only your shoes and socks come off, and trousers are rolled to the knee. You recline while your therapist works one foot at a time, pressing with thumbs along the sole and between the toes, circling the ankle and working up the calf with the palms.

The pressure is firm and specific, and your therapist will ask how it feels as they go. It is a short treatment that is easy to fit into a lunch break or at the end of a long day on your feet.

## focus

### What it focuses on

## faq

### Do I need to undress for a Thai foot massage?
No. You stay dressed; only your shoes and socks come off, and trousers are rolled up to the knee.

### Is Thai foot massage the same as reflexology?
It draws on reflexology-style pressure, working point by point across the soles with the thumbs, but it is a massage of the feet and lower legs rather than a reflexology session. We do not make any health claims for it.
