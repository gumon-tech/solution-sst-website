# Thai Massage in Greenwich

Lead: Thai massage is a traditional treatment done in loose clothing, without oil, in which the therapist presses along the body with palms, thumbs, forearms and elbows and moves you through gentle, yoga-style stretches. Our therapists learned it at Wat Pho in Bangkok, the temple school where the tradition is taught.

## What to expect

You stay dressed in loose, comfortable clothing for the whole treatment, so there is no oil and no undressing. Wear or bring something soft that you can stretch in; if you are not sure what to wear, ask us on WhatsApp when you book.

The treatment is done on our treatment bed. Your therapist works along the body in a steady rhythm, leaning in with body weight rather than pushing, and pausing to stretch an arm, a leg or the back the way a partner might in a yoga class. The pressure is firm by nature, and it is always yours to adjust: say "softer" or "deeper" at any point and the therapist changes it straight away.

Most people find it a very different feeling from an oil massage: more active, more stretching, and a strong sense of having been thoroughly moved and loosened by the end.

## focus

### What it focuses on
- The whole body, from feet to head, in a set sequence
- Pressing along the lines of the legs, back, arms and shoulders
- Assisted stretches for the hips, legs, back and shoulders
- Firm, steady pressure that you can turn up or down

### Good to know
- Done fully clothed, in loose clothing, with no oil
- Tell your therapist about any area to avoid or go gently on
- If you are pregnant, or have a health condition, an injury or a recent operation, tell us before you book so the team can confirm the right treatment. If in doubt, ask your doctor first.
- If you would rather have oil and long, flowing strokes, look at our Aromatherapy Massage

## faq

### Is Thai massage done with clothes on?
Yes. Thai massage is done fully clothed, in loose, comfortable clothing, and no oil is used. Wear or bring something soft you can stretch in, and ask us on WhatsApp if you are unsure.

### Does Thai massage hurt?
It is firm, and it should never be painful. Your therapist leans in gradually and watches how you respond, and you can ask for softer or deeper pressure at any moment. If you prefer a gentler treatment altogether, an Aromatherapy Massage may suit you better.

### How long is a Thai massage?
Choose the length that suits you from the options above; the longer option gives your therapist time to work more slowly through the whole sequence. Arrive a few minutes early so your full time is on the bed.
