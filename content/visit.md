In the town centre, on the DLR between Island Gardens and Greenwich station. About a 9-minute walk to us, and the Cutty Sark itself is about 11 minutes away.

DLR, plus Southeastern trains towards London Bridge and Cannon Street and Thameslink trains. About a 16-minute walk to us.

### Deptford station

## Three small things

### Arrive a few minutes early
It gives you time to settle in before your treatment.

### Tell us about your health
If you are pregnant, or have a health condition, an injury or a recent operation, let us know before you book so the team can confirm the right treatment. If in doubt, ask your doctor first.

### Running late?
Message us as soon as you know. The team will do its best for you, though we may need to shorten the treatment to keep the next guest on time.
